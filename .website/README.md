# OUT OF OFFICE

The grey is the office. The bosses are its supplies. The goal is to be out of it.

A top-down arena survival shooter. You are a unicorn whose horn is a prism: every shot is a rainbow. The Grey climbs out of inkwells, eats the colour out of the meadow and comes for you by the hundred, up to a thousand at once. Kill it and the colour it was holding bursts back into the ground.

**Controls.** **WASD** or **arrows** move, the **mouse** aims, hold to fire. **Shift**, **Q**, **E** or **right button** blinks through the grey, three charges. **P** pauses, **F** fullscreen, **M** sound. **Left** and **right** on the title pick EASY, NORMAL or HARD. On a level up, **click** a card; **R** rerolls once a level.

**The meadow is a resource.** Where the grey walks it leaves ash: you move at half speed on it, the grey a third faster. Kills and dying shots repaint it and the flowers grow back. ASH in the HUD is how much of the field is gone.

**Dispersion is the default weapon.** The PRISM fires seven bullets, one per band, each at its own speed, so every volley fans into a rainbow. Prism pickups shift the horn to one band: REDSHIFT is a hose, RAINBOW RAIL an instant beam, GREEN GOO puddles, BLUE FROST slows, YELLOW ARC chains lightning, ORANGE BURST is a shotgun.

**The office.** Every fifth wave a boss: the CENSOR fires rings of ink, the ERASER rubs out lanes, the STAPLER bites, the STAMP slams VOID where you stood, the SHREDDER pulls you in and spits moths. From wave 30 they come in pairs; every fortieth wave THE PHOTOCOPIER copies drabs off its edge.

**Perks and pickups.** Three cards a level from thirty-six perks, common to legend: stackable numbers, new rules (a magnet, regen on colour, a sharp horn, a repainting trail), a foal that fires, mirrored volleys, a glass horn that trades health for damage, night, endless ammo. Kills drop hearts, prisms, gold, a paint bomb, a nova, an hourglass, a shield, sugar rush, and shards: five make WHITE LIGHT. Twelve achievements and a best per difficulty persist.
